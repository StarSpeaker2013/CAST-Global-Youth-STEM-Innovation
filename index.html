About

About The Summit

The CAST Youth STEM Innovation and Entrepreneur Summit is organized by the Chinese Association for Science and Technology (CAST-USA), which is a national organization connecting scientists, entrepreneurs, and innovators across the United States.

CAST-USA has tens of thousands of members in more than 30 states across the United States and 16 chapters in major cities. CAST-USA is one of the most recognized organizations among Chinese professionals in the United States.

🚀 Through the summit, CAST connects students with top scientists, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders across the United States.

Two Tracks

Choose Your Competition Track

Each track has its own required materials and judging criteria.

Required

📄 Research Paper

  • Format: PDF or Word
  • Margin: 1 inch · Font: 12pt Times New Roman
  • Line Spacing: 1.5 · Pages: 2–8

Paper is for full explanation. Must include:

  • Project Objectives
  • Background & Motivation
  • Methodology / Technical Approach
  • Key Innovation
  • Results / Findings
  • Impact / Future Development
Optional

🖼️ Presentation Slides

  • Format: PDF or PPT · Max 12 slides

Slides are for concise summary. Must include:

  • Problem / Research Question
  • Approach
  • Innovation
  • Results
  • Impact
Optional

🎬 Project Video

  • Max 3 minutes

Demonstrate the project clearly and engagingly — show a prototype, process, or key results.

Required

📊 Pitch Deck

  • Format: PDF · Max 12 slides

Pitch Deck presents the idea clearly. Include:

  • Problem
  • Solution
  • Value Proposition
  • Target Market
  • Business Model
  • Execution / Go-to-Market
  • Impact
Required

📝 Business Proposal

  • Format: Word or PDF · Max 1000 words

Proposal explains feasibility. Include:

  • Why this problem matters
  • Why your solution works
  • Basic market understanding
  • Revenue / sustainability
  • Execution plan
Optional

🎬 Project Video

  • Max 3 minutes

Demonstrate the product, user experience, or key idea (e.g., prototype demo, usage scenario). Should complement — not repeat — the pitch deck.
